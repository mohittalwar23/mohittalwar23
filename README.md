## About Me

I am a final-year Electronics and Communication (IoT) student at IIIT Nagpur with a CGPA of 9.13. I work at the intersection of embedded firmware, real-time systems, and edge AI. Over the past year I interned at OLA Electric on their Embodied AI and ADAS team in Bangalore, working on vision-based ADAS features, multi-sensor calibration pipelines, hardware-accelerated GStreamer pipelines, and Fast-Lio SLAM. Before that I built Zephyr RTOS firmware for Nordic nRF52 boards at iHub Awadh, IIT Ropar.


---

## What I Work On

- **Embedded Firmware** — Zephyr RTOS, FreeRTOS, Nordic nRF52, ESP32-S3, NXP i.MX8MP
- **Real-Time Audio** — I2S, PDM/DMIC, DMA slab management, USB Audio Class (UAC2)
- **Edge AI / TinyML** — TensorFlow Lite Micro, INT8 quantisation, MFCC feature extraction
- **Robotics & ADAS** — ROS 2, Fast-Lio SLAM, Camera-Lidar calibration, CAN bus, Deepstream SDK
- **Protocols** — BLE, MQTT, CAN, I2C, SPI, UART
