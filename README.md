## About Me

I am a Firmware Engineer at **VIMAAN**, building embedded and edge systems for warehouse automation. 

I am also a **Google Summer of Code 2026 contributor to the Zephyr Project**, working on audio infrastructure for the mpipe multimedia framework. My contributions cover I2S and DMIC source elements, capability and buffer negotiation, pipeline lifecycle and xrun recovery, devicetree codec configuration, testing, and hardware validation on the NXP i.MX8MP EVK.

Previously, I worked at **Virya Autonomous Technologies** on SiMa.ai Modalix perception workloads, GStreamer pipelines, and a human-safety subsystem, and at **OLA Electric** on embedded AI and ADAS systems across NVIDIA Jetson, TI TDA4VM, and Qualcomm platforms.

I hold a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering (IoT) from **IIIT Nagpur**, with a CGPA of **9.13**.

## What I Work On

- **Firmware & RTOS:** C, C++, Zephyr RTOS, FreeRTOS, device integration, state machines, structured logging
- **Embedded Linux & Build:** Embedded Linux, Yocto-based environments, Docker, CMake, West, CI/CD
- **Audio & Multimedia:** I2S, DMIC/PDM, GStreamer, DeepStream, capability and buffer negotiation

- **Platforms & Interfaces:** NXP i.MX8MP, TI TDA4VM, Qualcomm QCS5430, CAN, USB, I2C, SPI, UART
